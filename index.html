STAGE 1: CURRICULUM & MISCONCEPTION BLUEPRINTING

Mapping KSSR Year 6 Science (Unit 4) to Diagnostic Distractors

Each of the 10 items in questions.js was reverse-engineered from documented Year 6 student misconceptions—specifically confusing Commensalism (+ / 0) with Parasitism (+ / -) in epiphytes (Bird's Nest Fern on Oil Palm) and marine hitchhikers (Remora on Shark). Every distractor is mapped to a specific misconceptionTag.

STAGE 2: CUSTOM MULTIMEDIA (SVG DIAGRAMS & WEB AUDIO)

Dual-Coding Visual Ecological Diagrams & Audio Narration

To support visual learners and reduce reading fatigue (Mayer's Multimedia Principle), custom scalable vector graphics (SVG) diagrams with explicit [+ / 0 / -] callout badges were embedded for all 10 scenarios alongside a Web Audio API sound synthesizer and Web Speech API field narration reader.

STAGE 3: ANXIETY-REDUCING GAMIFICATION & FEEDBACK ENGINE

Mindful Self-Pace Mode, Power-Ups & Redemption Queue

To directly solve the classroom test-anxiety problem identified in Question 1, punitive speed-countdown timers were replaced with a Mindful Self-Pace Toggle, 3 strategic Power-Ups (50/50 Filter, 2x Bio-Boost, Streak Shield), immediate Diagnostic Coaching Drawers, and an automatic Redemption Lab Round.

STAGE 4: TEACHER FORMATIVE ANALYTICS & SMARTBOARD ANONYMIZER

Closing the Formative Loop with Real-Time Misconception Data

Built the Teacher Misconception Analytics Dashboard featuring a Question Accuracy Heatmap, Distractor Breakdown Inspector, CSV Exporter, and a one-click Hide Student Names (Smartboard Safe Mode) button so the teacher can review tricky questions without embarrassing struggling learners.
